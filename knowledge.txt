## CLINIC OVERVIEW

Kestrel Family Clinic is a fictional general-practice clinic used to
demonstrate the MediDesk assistant. All names, plans and records in this file
are synthetic.

## OPENING HOURS

The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 8:30am to 9:00pm, and Saturday from
8:30am to 1:00pm. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
Registration closes 30 minutes before closing time.

## CANCELLATION AND NO-SHOW POLICY

Patients must cancel at least 24 hours before their appointment. Late
cancellations and no-shows are charged a 20 dollar fee. Patients may
reschedule once without charge if they give at least 24 hours' notice.

## INSURANCE AND PANEL PLANS

The clinic accepts AsterCare and Bluepeak panel plans. Patients show their
member card at registration and the clinic files the claim. Patients without
a panel plan pay at the counter and receive an itemised receipt.

## VACCINATIONS

Flu vaccines are available as walk-ins Monday to Friday from 2:00pm to
5:00pm. Childhood vaccinations need an appointment, and parents should bring
the child's health booklet.

## FRONT DESK HANDLING OF RECORD REQUESTS

Front desk staff do not discuss diagnoses, medications or test results.
Requests about a patient's record are passed to the duty clinician.
